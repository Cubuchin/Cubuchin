# Public Portfolio Policy

This repository is designed to demonstrate professional capabilities while protecting proprietary work, confidential information, and the privacy of people and organizations connected to that work.

## Purpose

The portfolio may describe:

- The problem a project addresses
- My role, responsibilities, and decision-making approach
- High-level workflows and design principles
- Tools and capabilities used in a general, non-sensitive way
- Publicly available media, education, and research outputs
- Public-facing product views and screenshots containing only synthetic staging data
- Lessons learned that do not reveal protected information

## Information That Stays Private

The portfolio does not publish:

- Source code from private or proprietary projects
- Credentials, tokens, secrets, keys, environment variables, or deployment configuration
- Private repository content or commit history
- Internal architecture that would create unnecessary security exposure
- Assessment questions, scoring logic, weighting, recommendation logic, profiling methods, or simulation templates
- Client, employee, subscriber, participant, patient, or organizational data
- Employer-confidential records, incidents, procedures, screenshots, diagrams, or system details
- Unpublished research notes or third-party material that is not mine to distribute

## InPhishion Disclosure Standard

Public descriptions of InPhishion are limited to the venture's problem space, verified public availability and pilot stage, implemented high-level workflows, sanitized visuals, design principles, and my role in directing the work. As of September 2026, the public site and authenticated application are live, and controlled organizational pilots are in preparation. Public availability does not establish scientific validation or measured customer outcomes.

They should not be interpreted as claims that:

- Every planned capability is complete, production-approved, or deployed for customers
- The assessment model has been scientifically validated
- A behavioral result is a diagnosis or fixed judgment about a person
- The system can guarantee or definitively predict future behavior
- Client outcomes or production performance have been established

InPhishion-related language should favor terms such as **provisional hypothesis**, **directional evidence**, **protective behavior**, **reporting behavior**, **analyst review**, and **aggregate reporting**.

## Security and Ethics

Public examples should not provide instructions that meaningfully improve harmful phishing, impersonation, credential theft, evasion, or unauthorized access.

Simulation descriptions remain high level and emphasize controlled, approved, fictional, or organization-owned contexts. Real-brand mimicry, clone domains, spoof-style identities, and cloned login pages are outside the public portfolio's scope.

## Accuracy and Editorial Control

All public descriptions should:

- Distinguish completed work from planned or in-progress work
- Avoid fabricated metrics, incidents, testimonials, citations, or outcomes
- Use careful language when evidence is incomplete
- Preserve human review and final editorial control over AI-assisted work
- Be updated or removed when they no longer represent the work accurately

## Contact and Corrections

Questions or correction requests can be raised through the contact links on the main profile page. Sensitive information should never be submitted through a public GitHub issue.
